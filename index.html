      About Singhanon

      Singhanon is a research prototype: an Aklanon–English dictionary built to give secondary learners and teachers a fast, expandable reference for the Aklanon dialect.

      Entries are adapted from Salas Reyes, Zorc & Prado (1969), Aklanon to English, a Peace Corps dictionary (ERIC ED145704).
